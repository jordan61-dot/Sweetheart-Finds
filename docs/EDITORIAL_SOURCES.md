## Evidence and policy

PR TIMES https://prtimes.jp/main/html/kiyaku defines reporting persons broadly to include using published information to disseminate information on one's own or another medium (basic terms Article3(8)). Its company terms Article7(3) grant free nonexclusive use of corporate content for reporting, and reporting terms Article3(1) allow reporting use. Guest information gathering/private use/quotation is separately allowed. These records are bounded, manually researched, original editorial reference notes with attribution and source links; this assessment does not assert a bulk catalog harvesting or artwork license. No PR TIMES artwork is included.

The Smithsonian indexed primary record explicitly labels metadata CC0. Direct retrieval returned HTTP520 during research, which is noted in provenance. The Strong Museum record is a specific factual reference (object112.5934); its copyrighted photograph and narrative are omitted. Product names/identifiers are used descriptively; no museum or manufacturer endorsement is asserted.

MGA terms now explicitly prohibit automated gathering, and PRNewswire terms restrict database storage/abstract reuse; no collector or dataset from either service is delivered. Open Library is used only for a bounded user-requested title lookup, with cache retained. Three selected Parragon publication identities are included, while unofficial/unidentified coloring books are omitted.
